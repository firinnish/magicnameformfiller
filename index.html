Atlantia Name Submission

This webpage generates a correctly-filled-out copy of Atlantia's official individual name submission form PDF. It only formats what you enter into the form and helps you to navigate through the requirements - it does not check your name for conflict or registerability. Please give the filled-out PDF to a warranted herald, who will send it on to Ibis if it passes a conflict check.

Note: on a wide enough screen, this page shows a live preview of the PDF as you fill out the form. This won't work on mobile or in Safari, but the PDF download should still work at the end.

If you gave a Society name when you signed up for an SCA membership, this does NOT mean you registered an SCA name. You can give an unregistered name for your membership card. You have only registered your name if you registered it with the College of Heralds and paid for the submission. If you're not sure whether you registered a name, try searching the Ordinary & Armorial.

If you're not sure whether your name was returned at Kingdom level or Laurel level, consider the timeline; Kingdom would have returned your name a month or two after you submitted it, whereas Laurel would have returned your name six months to a year later. If your name was returned on an LOAR, it was returned by Laurel.

If you don't have an SCA name yet, put the name you are submitting as your "SCA Name".

We ask for your contact details because (among other reasons) when you register a name, that means that nobody in the future can register a very similar name without your permission. People might want to send you a text or email to ask if it's OK to register a similar name to the one you've taken. If you don't want this to happen, you can issue a blanket permission to conflict so that people don't need to ask you.

Name Processing Preferences

When you submit a name to the College, sometimes they can accept it as-is, sometimes they have to return it to you so you can try again, and sometimes there's a small tweak that can turn an unregisterable name into a registerable one. For example, if you made a small grammar mistake in forming a patronymic, the College can fix your grammar mistake and accept your name. What would you prefer?

Please specify the meaning/sound/spelling/language culture that you want us to preserve. For example: "The name should sound like Katie but the spelling can change to Katy or Catie as necessary", "The name should use a spelling with a J in it", "the name should be Norse", "the name should have a meaning relating to lions or cats", "my dad's name was Paul so please keep the patronymic meaning daughter of Paul". That way, if the heralds have to change your name, we can keep what is important to you.

Note: The heralds do not know, or care, what gender you are. We only need to know what gender you want this name to be, so that we can make sure the grammar is correct.
